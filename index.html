Leaf in, diagnosis out: the idea behind A.P.D.A.N.A.R.

Autonomous Plant Disease and Nutrient Analyzer Robot

Aug 2024 – present · Robotics, mobile and machine learning

A farm robot that photographs leaves, classifies plant disease with a neural network and acts on the result. It won 2nd place at the National World Robot Olympiad and addresses 5 of the 13 UN Sustainable Development Goals tracked in the competition.

  1. CaptureAndroid app (Java) photographs the leaf
  2. ClassifyCNN built in TensorFlow/Keras
  3. CommandPyQt5 desktop app controls camera and robot
  4. ActArduino UNO robot works the farm
  • Trained a CNN and artificial neural network to classify plant diseases from leaf images.
  • Connected the phone, server and robot over sockets.
  • Built the Arduino robot that receives commands from the server.
  • Moved the server onto the phone so farmers without a personal computer can still process images, saving energy and money.
